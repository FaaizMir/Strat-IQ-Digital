Our Process

How We
Do It.

We don't start writing content on day one. Every engagement starts with a deep audit and intent research — then we build a strategy around what will actually move the needle.

01 The SEO Audit

We start by looking under the hood. We find the technical errors, content gaps, and cannibalization issues that are stopping you from ranking — giving us a clear roadmap before we write a single word or change a single setting.

02 Market & Intent Research

We don't just look for high-volume keywords — we look for high-intent ones. The words your customers use when they're ready to buy, not just browse. This is the foundation every piece of content is built on.

03 Content Deployment

Our content team creates the pages, guides, and articles needed to capture the market. Everything is built to be the single best answer on the web for that topic — not just keyword-stuffed filler that ranks for a week and disappears.

04 Continuous Optimization

The search market changes constantly. We monitor your rankings and competitors daily — refreshing content, adjusting internal linking, and responding to algorithm shifts to keep you at the top long after the initial push.

Answers to Everything

Frequently
Asked.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from brands starting or scaling their organic search presence.


Have more? Contact us

How do I find the right SEO agency for my business?

Look for an agency that talks about ROI, not just rankings. The right partner understands your business goals and builds a strategy around them — not one that sends you monthly keyword reports and calls it done.

Do you offer specialized Shopify SEO?

Yes. We know exactly how to handle Shopify's technical structure — collection page indexing, duplicate content from filter parameters, URL hierarchy, and more. It's also fully integrated with our Shopify development services.

What's the benefit of local SEO?

If you serve a specific area, local SEO ensures you show up when nearby customers search for what you offer. It's the most efficient way to win your local market against national competitors with bigger budgets.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show movement quickly. A full content and organic SEO strategy typically takes 3–6 months to show significant compounding growth — and continues to compound long after that. It's a long-term investment, not a quick fix.

Is your content written by humans or AI?

Our strategy is led by experienced SEO professionals. While we use tools for data and research, the content itself is written to build genuine human-to-human authority — the kind that earns trust and actually ranks long term.

Do you also handle content audits on existing sites?

Yes. We audit existing content to find pages that are underperforming, cannibalizing each other, or dragging down your domain. We then refresh, rewrite, or redirect — turning weak pages into assets instead of liabilities.
